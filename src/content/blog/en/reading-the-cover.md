Try covering the title for a second. Where does your eye go first? When we designed this cover, we wanted you to notice the small gap between two hands. That’s where two people are working on the same thing.

## Follow the yellow

Look just at the yellow shapes, then at the red on the opposite side. Yellow draws attention to the meeting point. Red gives the other figure weight. Against the charcoal background, the white outlines of the hands are easy to trace even in a small image.

## Try it with the cover

Open “Inside the cover” in the gallery. Look at the space between the hands in the close-up, move on to the ink colours, then turn to the finished cover. With the JIGSAW lettering back in the frame, does your eye follow the same route?
